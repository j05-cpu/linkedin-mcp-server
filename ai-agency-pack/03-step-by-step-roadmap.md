# 🗺️ 30-Day Step-by-Step Execution Roadmap for Solo Agency Founder

Is roadmap ko follow karke aap **Day 1 se Day 30** ke andar bina single penny spend kiye apni AI Agency ki pehli 2-3 paying clients land kar sakte ho.

---

## 📅 Week 1: Foundation & Portfolio Proof (Days 1 - 7)

- **Day 1: Setup Environment**
  - Fork/clone this repo (`linkedin-mcp-server`) & ensure local node/npm works.
  - Obtain free Gemini API key from Google AI Studio.
  - Setup a free Telegram Bot via `@BotFather` for testing alert features.

- **Day 2-3: Build 2 Sample Micro-Automations**
  - Micro-Automation 1: Automated LinkedIn Post + Gemini Image Generator (Test using this repo).
  - Micro-Automation 2: Playwright scraper that extracts leads/data into a clean CSV/JSON.

- **Day 4-5: Document Proof of Work**
  - Screen-record short 30-60 second Loom / WebM videos showing the automation in action (Terminal output, published post, Telegram alert).
  - Upload these videos to a free Google Drive folder or YouTube Unlisted link.

- **Day 6-7: Profile Optimization**
  - Update LinkedIn Profile headline:
    *"I build custom AI & Micro-Automations for Tech Founders & Agencies | $0 Overhead, 10x Efficiency"*
  - Write a LinkedIn post detailing how you built an automated LinkedIn scheduler in 1 evening.

---

## 📅 Week 2: Targeted Outreach to Tech-Aware Prospects (Days 8 - 14)

- **Target Profiles**: Tech Founders, Agency Owners, Indie Hackers, Growth Leads who ALREADY talk about AI/Automation on LinkedIn or X/Twitter.
- **Goal**: Reach out to 10 qualified prospects per day (70 total per week).

- **Daily Routine**:
  - Spend 20 mins engaging (thoughtful comments) on target clients' LinkedIn posts.
  - Send 10 personalized Cold DMs (Use templates from [`04-sales-and-outreach-templates.md`](./04-sales-and-outreach-templates.md)).

---

## 📅 Week 3: Demo Calls, Proposals & Closing (Days 15 - 21)

- **Goal**: Convert 5-7 DM replies into 15-minute live screen-share Demos.
- **In the Call**:
  - Don't sell "AI jargon". Show the working code/video!
  - Ask: *"Which manual process is taking up most of your team's time every week?"*
  - Pitch 1 tailored Micro-Automation solution for $200 setup + optional maintenance.

- **Closing**: Send the 1-page proposal template with clear deliverables and 24-48h turnaround time.

---

## 📅 Week 4: Delivery, Testimonial & Retainer Scaling (Days 22 - 30)

- **Deliver fast**: Finish the micro-automation setup in 48 hours.
- **Over-deliver**: Add a free bonus (e.g., Telegram instant error alert or custom reporting dashboard).
- **Ask for Review**: Get a text/video testimonial for LinkedIn social proof.
- **Pitch Monthly Retainer**: Offer $50/mo monitoring & update support.
